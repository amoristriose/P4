About Me

Curious, expressive, and always learning.

Hello! I'm Hawa, an RPL student with interests that go beyond technology and programming. I enjoy exploring different forms of creative and analytical work, including writing, literature, teaching, and digital projects. I like learning new things, experimenting with ideas, and turning what I learn into something meaningful and tangible.

Writing is one of the ways I enjoy expressing myself and exploring ideas. I have an interest in essay writing, creative writing, and literature, and I enjoy working with words to communicate thoughts, perspectives, and stories. At the same time, my background in technology has taught me to approach problems with structure, logic, and attention to detail.

I also enjoy teaching and helping others understand things that may initially seem difficult. Whether it is explaining a concept, organizing information, or working through a problem together, I find meaning in making things clearer and more accessible. These experiences have helped me develop communication, patience, adaptability, and a better understanding of different perspectives.

Overall, I see myself as someone who is curious, reflective, and always willing to learn. I enjoy bringing together logic and creativity, technology and language, and independent thinking and collaboration. Through my work and projects, I hope to keep developing my skills while creating things that are not only useful, but also thoughtful and meaningful.
